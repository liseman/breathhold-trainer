SUPPORT

What is this?

7MW is the dead-simple seven-minute workout: one START button, twelve bodyweight exercises (30 seconds on, 10 seconds off), voice-guided. That's the whole app.

Privacy policy

7MW collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and the app makes no network requests. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device. There is nothing to delete because nothing is stored.
